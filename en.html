01 — Real segmentation output

This jaw is not an illustration.

The model on screen was reconstructed directly from the segmentation mask our nnU-Net v2 produced on a real CBCT volume. Mandible, maxilla, teeth, nerve canal and maxillary sinus were each labelled separately.

02 — Critical structure

Inferior alveolar nerve canal.

When an implant is placed less than 1 mm from the mandibular canal, neurosensory disturbance is unavoidable. The system segments this pathway and raises an automatic alert at a 2 mm safety margin.

Peña-Cardelles et al., 2025 — Med Oral Patol Oral Cir Bucal

03 — Maxillary sinus

The risk zone of the upper jaw.

Sinus perforation is the most frequent complication in maxillary surgery. The system extracts the sinus boundary and alerts at 1.5 mm. Unlike existing solutions, both jaws are covered on one platform.

Measurement note

Across the full validation set the mean Dice is 0.7405. The difference does not come from the model but from the completeness of the reference labels: in part of the ToothFairy2 volumes some structures are never annotated. In those cases the model may segment the structure correctly, yet with no reference to compare against the Dice is recorded as zero. The maxillary sinus is annotated in only 13 of 96 cases and the maxilla in 36; the model still segmented the sinus in 15 of the unannotated cases.

Each structure is therefore reported only over the cases in which it is present in the reference — the standard approach for partially annotated datasets. Both figures are stated openly here.

07 — Mixed-reality layer

Built on HoloLens 2 with Unity and MRTK3, the interface renders the step data arriving over UDP from the control station as a holographic panel that gently follows the surgeon's gaze.
